# What customers actually want — evidence from a competitor's community

**Source:** WhatsApp export of *"Traffic Rules app 1"*, 21–26 Nov 2026 (242 member
messages, 105 senders, 62 system events). It is **not our group** — it belongs to
the competitor app *Traffic Rules* (Play Store `com.traffic.rules`, admin
"Eugene"). Phone numbers are deliberately not reproduced here.

---

## 1. The competitor, as observed

| Item | What the chat shows |
|---|---|
| Price | **RWF 5,000 for 6 months**, one-off |
| Payment | Manual **MoMo Pay 590934**, then *"call this number and we help you"* |
| Free tier | None visible — pay first, then use |
| Funnel | The **app itself auto-adds users to the WhatsApp group** ("you came here through the app") |
| Scale | 4+ groups, "1025 each", this one grown **1024 → 1500**; plus a "VIP" group |
| Channels | Play Store app, YouTube channel, WhatsApp channel |
| Community style | Admin-only announcements mixed with member chaos, 8 reposts of the same rules block, **12 people removed by admin, 13 left** in 5 days |

**Read:** they have solved *distribution* (5,000+ people reached) and *payment*
(manual MoMo), and nothing else. There is almost no teaching in the group.

## 2. What people kept asking for (verbatim intent, translated)

**A. The actual study material — the single loudest request**
- *"Nobody has the questions and answers for traffic rules — give them to me."*
- *"I'd like help getting the questions and answers."*
- *"Is there anyone with the PDF?"* → *"Give me the PDF"* → *"thanks"*
- *"How do I get the rules you have? Send them to me."*
- *"Where do you get these rules you study? Why don't you post them here?"*

**B. Trust**
- *"Who will give me an app we can rely on?"*
- *"Be careful believing middlemen — they'll eat your money and mislead you."*
- *"I paid, tell me and get me a motorcycle code."*

**C. Process confusion — app codes vs Irembo codes**
- *"Permit codes for category B are out — go to Irembo and they give you a code."*
- *"I want a code for A."*
- *"How does one get a provisional licence? I want one."*
- *"When you've studied and feel ready, you go to Irembo and ask for a code near where you live."*
- *"I came here thinking you help people learn so they get a provisional, but I don't understand what you're talking about."*

**D. Account basics**
- *"If someone forgets their password, how are they helped?"* (answer: *"call me"*)
- *"My phone broke — how do I find it?"* (i.e. access lost with the device)

**E. Live teaching**
- *"Admin, put this group on a live line."*
- *"Talk about why the group was created, stop insulting others."*

**F. New exam format**
- *"The exam system is back — who wants codes to use it?"*
- *"And the automatic exams they brought to the province?"*

## 3. What they hated (and we must not repeat)

- **Being added without consent** — *"Did you add us because we wanted it? Did you ask us?"*
- **A group with no substance** — *"The problem isn't the group. The problem is there's nothing useful in it."*
- **Mass removals and admin spam** — 12 removed, 8 identical reposts.
- **No answers** — questions about permits, passwords and content went unanswered or were met with *"call me"*.
- 53 of 242 messages were **media-only** (screenshots of questions) — people are
  passing material around because there is no proper source.

---

## 4. What this means for Inzira

### Confirmed strengths (keep pushing)
1. **Free tier.** They have none. Our 60 questions + 24 pictures + 1 mock is a
   better first experience than "pay 5,000 before you see anything".
2. **Opt-in by design.** A school link/QR unlocks only for whoever opens it —
   the exact opposite of the auto-add that this community resents.
3. **The content exists and is offline.** Their users are begging for Q&A and
   PDFs; we have 394 verified questions with explanations, cached offline.
4. **No manual activation.** Their funnel is "pay → call a human". Ours is
   "open link → studying in 10 seconds".

### Gaps this chat exposes (ranked by value ÷ effort)

| # | Gap | Why it matters | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Printable / shareable PDF booklet** (questions + signs) | The most repeated request in the chat, twice explicitly. Also our best offline marketing asset — a school can print it, a student can share it on WhatsApp. | Medium (we already have a print pipeline) |
| 2 | **"How to get your provisional licence" page** (study → Irembo code → exam → 12/20 → RWF 5,000) | The #1 source of confusion is app codes vs Irembo codes. Owning this explanation makes us the trustworthy guide, and it is free SEO/WhatsApp content. | Low |
| 3 | **Explicit "we don't sell permit codes" line** | Protects us from being mistaken for the middlemen this community warns about. | Trivial |
| 4 | **Password / device recovery story** | They answer this with "call me". We can answer with "your progress is on your phone; an account only adds sync" + a real reset. | Low–medium |
| 5 | **Share cards** (a question as an image) | 53 media-only messages show organic sharing already happens. Give it a clean, branded form. | Low |
| 6 | **A channel, not a group** | They run noisy 1,500-person groups with mass removals. A broadcast channel + weekly Q&A is the calm version. | Ops, not code |
| 7 | **Weekly live session** | Explicitly requested ("put this group on a live line"). | Ops |

### Pricing read
Their anchor is **RWF 5,000 / 6 months, paid up front, no trial**. That is a
useful reference in two directions:
- **B2C later:** we can sit at RWF 2,000–3,000 / 6 months *with* a free tier and
  still look cheaper and safer.
- **B2B now:** "less than the app everyone already pays 5,000 for, per student,
  with a report on who is ready" is a strong line for a driving school.

### Risk this chat raises
Two separate mentions of a **new/returned exam system** and "automatic exams"
suggest the national theory exam may have changed format. If so, our bank (built
from one PDF) may be out of date in *shape*, not just in answer keys. This makes
the instructor review more urgent, and adds one question to ask them:
*"Has the exam moved to computer-based testing, and did the question format change?"*

## 5. What we should build next (recommendation)

1. **PDF study booklet** (`assets/booklet/`) — all 394 questions + signs +
   explanations, print-ready A5/A4, Kinyarwanda. Doubles as the school handout
   and the WhatsApp forward.
2. **"Uko ubona provisoire" page** — a short, honest, 5-step explainer with the
   Irembo/permit clarification and a "we don't sell codes" line.
3. **Share-a-question card** — one tap → branded image of a question, for
   WhatsApp status.

All three are cheap, use what we already have, and answer requests people made
in this exact chat.

## 6. Ethics / privacy guardrail

The export contains ~105 real phone numbers. Do **not** message or add these
people: unsolicited marketing from a scraped export is both against WhatsApp's
terms and against Rwanda's data-protection law, and it would destroy the trust
position we are trying to own. Use the chat for **insight only**.
