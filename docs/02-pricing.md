# Pricing sheet — pilot

All prices in Rwandan francs (RWF), VAT-exclusive unless stated. Validity: pilot period (first 90 days).

## 1. Individual learners (B2C)

| Pack | Price | What they get | Positioning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 0 | 2 mock exams, all signs, 30 practice questions | Hook |
| **Premium 60** | **2,500** | Unlimited mocks for 60 days, all 257 questions, weak-topic drills, offline | "Half the price of one re-sit" |
| **Crash 48h** | **1,000** | 48 hours unlimited, exam simulator, 40-question rapid drill | Sold the day they book a slot |
| **Pass guarantee** | **4,000** | Premium + re-sit voucher if they complete the course and fail | Only after 85%+ pass rate is proven |

Rules for the guarantee:

- Complete at least 6 mock exams with a score of 14/20 or better on the last two.
- Sit the real exam within 60 days of purchase.
- Show the official result slip.
- One claim per learner. Voucher covers the RWF 5,000 re-sit fee only.

## 2. Driving schools (B2B)

| Pack | Price | Unit price | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 50,000 | 1,000 / student | 50 seats, 90 days, basic cohort report |
| **Standard** | 120,000 | 1,200 / student | 100 seats, 6 months, dashboard, co-branded QR |
| **Partner** | 250,000 | 1,250 / student | 200 seats, 12 months, dashboard, white-label option, quarterly review |

- Seats are transferable within the school but expire at the end of the term.
- Revenue share: **25%** back to the school on any B2C upgrade sold through its code.
- Payment: 50% on signature, 50% on seat activation.
- Pilot schools get the first 100 seats free in exchange for pass-rate data.

## 6. Discount policy

- No discount below RWF 1,000/seat without founder approval.
- Referral: both sides get 7 days of premium, not cash.
- Never discount the guarantee — change the terms instead.

## 7. What to say when they say "it's free elsewhere"

> "Free tools give you questions. We tell you whether you will pass, and your school can see it. One re-sit costs RWF 5,000. This costs RWF 2,500 and works without internet."

Then stop talking. Let the price sit against the re-sit fee.
