# 90-day launch checklist

Every task has an owner and a done-test. If a week's done-test fails, fix it before moving on.

## Weeks 1–2 — Validate

- [ ] Read `01-business-plan.md` and `02-pricing.md`; decide the two pilot schools.
- [ ] Get the current official road-code booklet (RNP / traffic police) — the PDF bank is the base, not the final authority.
- [ ] Interview 20 learners: what they used, what they paid, why they failed (if they did).
- [ ] Interview 5 schools: how many students, current pass rate, what they would pay.
- [ ] Recruit one driving instructor as content reviewer (paid per reviewed batch).
- [ ] **Done-test:** 5 schools say yes to a free pilot; 1 instructor signs a review agreement.

## Weeks 3–4 — Prepare the pilot

- [ ] Deploy the app to a real URL (see README).
- [ ] Load the 257-question bank; instructor reviews the first 100 questions.
- [ ] Print 200 co-branded QR cards for the two schools.
- [ ] Set up a WhatsApp Business number and a simple spreadsheet for results.
- [ ] Write the 4-message onboarding sequence (below).
- [ ] **Done-test:** both schools have codes, QR cards, and a named contact.

## Weeks 5–8 — Run the pilot

- [ ] Onboard students at enrolment; record who gets a code (treatment) and who does not (control).
- [ ] Send the day-1, day-3, day-7 and exam-eve messages.
- [ ] Check the cohort report every Friday; call any school with under 30% activation.
- [ ] Collect real exam results within 30 days.
- [ ] Fix the top 5 confusing questions flagged by students.
- [ ] **Done-test:** 60+ activated students, 3+ mocks each on average, results for 80% of sitters.

## Weeks 9–10 — Prove

- [ ] Compute pass rate: treatment vs control.
- [ ] Write a one-page result sheet (no school names without permission).
- [ ] Record 3 short testimonial videos with consent.
- [ ] Decide go / fix / stop using the §11 criteria in the business plan.
- [ ] **Done-test:** a number you are willing to put on a poster.

## Weeks 11–13 — Sell

- [ ] Sign 10 schools at the Starter or Standard tier.
- [ ] Launch the referral loop (7 days premium for both sides).
- [ ] Launch the RWF 1,000 crash pack.
- [ ] Turn on MoMo/Airtel collection.
- [ ] Publish the result sheet on WhatsApp, TikTok and at exam centres.
- [ ] **Done-test:** RWF 1M collected or contracted.

## WhatsApp onboarding sequence (Kinyarwanda)

**Day 0 — welcome**

> Murakaza neza kuri *Inzira*. Nta internet ukeneye.
> Kanda hano ushyire app kuri telefone: [link]
> Kode y'ishuri ryawe: [CODE]
> Tangira ikizamini kimwe uyu munsi.

**Day 1 — first win**

> Wamaze ikizamini? Niba utaratsinda 12/20, ntibyago — ni yo mpamvu turi hano.
> Kora "Kwiga" ku ngingo yawe nto kugeza ugera kuri 14/20.

**Day 3 — accountability**

> Ufite iminsi 3. Abanyeshuri benshi batsinda nyuma y'ibizamini 6.
> Ubu uri kuri [X]/20. Komeza.

**Exam eve**

> Ejo ni ikizamini. Rya neza, ruhuka, kandi ujye ku kizamini n'indangamuntu.
> Urasanzwe uri kuri 14/20 — witeguye.

**After the exam**

> Byagenze bite? Duhe amanota yawe (yatsinze/yatsinzwe gusa).
> Ibi bidufasha gufasha abandi.

## Weekly metrics sheet

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | students with 1+ mock / seats issued | ≥ 70% |
| Engagement | average mocks per active student | ≥ 3 |
| Readiness | average readiness score at exam date | ≥ 70 |
| Pass rate (treatment) | passed / sat | ≥ 80% |
| Pass rate (control) | passed / sat | baseline |
| Lift | treatment − control | ≥ +10 pts |
| CAC | spend / activated students | ≤ RWF 800 |
| Revenue | collected, not invoiced | — |

## Go / no-go

Continue only if **all three** are true:

1. Activation ≥ 50%
2. Lift ≥ +10 percentage points
3. At least one school signs a paid seat licence at RWF 1,000+

Otherwise: fix activation first (it is almost always onboarding, not product), then re-test. Do not add features to solve a distribution problem.
