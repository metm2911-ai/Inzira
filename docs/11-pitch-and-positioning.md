# Gap analysis & pitch — what the competitor's users lack, and how we say it

Companion to `docs/10-customer-insights.md` (evidence from the competitor's
WhatsApp community, 21–26 Nov 2026). This file is a **sales tool**: the gaps,
who they hurt, and the exact words to use.

---

## 1. The gap, in one table

| # | What their users lack | Evidence from the chat | What Inzira does about it | Can we say it today? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Anything to study for free** | No free tier; pay 5,000 first, then "call me" | 60 questions + 24 pictures + 1 mock, free, no account | ✅ yes |
| 2 | **The questions and answers themselves** | *"Nobody has the Q&A — give them to me"*, *"give me the PDF"* ×2 | 394 questions with explanations, offline | ✅ yes |
| 3 | **A clear answer on permits** | *"I want a code for A"*, *"how do I get a provisional?"*, *"I don't understand what you're talking about"* | Not built yet — this is the gap to close | ⚠️ needs the explainer page |
| 4 | **Trust** | *"Who will give me an app we can rely on?"*, *"middlemen will eat your money"* | Free to check, no payment before use, no auto-add | ✅ yes |
| 5 | **Consent** | *"Did you ask us before adding us?"* — app auto-adds to WhatsApp group | School link/QR unlocks only for whoever opens it | ✅ yes |
| 6 | **Knowing if you're ready** | Nothing; they guess, then fail and pay 5,000 again | Readiness score, weak-topic drills, mock with 12/20 pass mark | ✅ yes |
| 7 | **Account recovery** | *"If someone forgets their password?"* → *"call me"* | No account needed to study; progress stays on the phone | ✅ yes (say it plainly) |
| 8 | **Teaching, not noise** | *"There's nothing useful in it"*, *"put this group on a live line"* | Instructor reviewer mode, weak-topic drill, cohort report for schools | ✅ partly |
| 9 | **A printable/forwardable copy** | 53 of 242 messages were screenshots of questions | Not built yet | ⚠️ needs the PDF booklet |
| 10 | **A school that benefits** | Students pay 5,000 to a stranger; the school gets no data | Seat pricing + monthly cohort report + 25% revenue share | ✅ yes |

**The core gap in one sentence:** their product is a *paywall in front of a PDF*,
wrapped in a noisy group. Nobody is teaching, nobody is measuring, and nobody is
answering the two questions people actually ask — *"where do I get the material?"*
and *"how do I get my permit?"*

---

## 2. Positioning line

> **Inzira** — *"Menya niba witeguye, mbere yo kwishyura ikizamini."*
> Know whether you are ready, before you pay for the exam.

For schools:

> *"Your students already pay RWF 5,000 each to an app that gives you nothing.
> Inzira costs a fifth of that per seat and tells you who is ready."*

Why this line works against them: it does not compete on "more questions". It
competes on **certainty** and **evidence**, which their product cannot produce.

## 3. The student pitch (30 seconds, Kinyarwanda)

> Ntukishyure mbere yo kubona ikintu.
> Kuri Inzira, ibibazo **60 n'ibisubizo** urabibona **ku buntu** — harimo
> **amafoto y'ibimenyetso** by'umuhanda.
> Ntukenera internet, kandi **nta muntu ukuyishyira mu group**.
> Iyo witeguye, urabona **amanota yawe** n'ibibazo unanirwa mo.
>
> **Ntabwo tugurisha code ya provisoire.** Code yawe uyikura kuri **Irembo** —
> twe tugufasha kwiga mbere y'uko uyijyana ku kizamini.

English version:

> Don't pay before you see anything. On Inzira, **60 questions with answers are
> free**, including **road-sign photos**. No internet needed, and **nobody adds
> you to a group**. When you're ready you see **your score** and **the topics you
> fail**. **We don't sell permit codes** — you get that from Irembo; we make sure
> you pass when you do.

### The four sentences that do the work
1. **"Urabibona ku buntu."** — they must pay 5,000 before seeing a single question.
2. **"Nta muntu ukuyishyira mu group."** — the most resented thing in their community.
3. **"Ntukenera internet."** — real for their users (data cost, patchy coverage).
4. **"Ntabwo tugurisha code."** — the honesty line that separates us from middlemen.

## 5. The school pitch (owner / director)

**Open with their money, not our features:**

> *"Buri mwaka abanyeshuri bawe bishyura 5,000 Frw kuri app idahari, nta
> makuru uhabwa. Niba bananirwa, bongera kwishyura. Nta nta data ufite."*
> Every year your students each pay RWF 5,000 to an app you don't control, get no
> data back, and re-sit at 5,000 when they fail.

**Then the offer:**

| | What they get |
|---|---|
| Price | **RWF 1,000–1,200 / seat** — a fifth of the app they already pay for |
| Unlock | One link/QR at enrolment — no typing, works offline |
| Data | Monthly report: seats used, mocks taken, average score, **who is predicted ready**, weakest topics |
| Money | **25% revenue share** back to the school |
| Risk | **8-week free pilot**, 100 seats, in exchange for pass-rate numbers |

**The close:** *"Ntugura ubusa. Duha abanyeshuri bawe ibibazo 394 n'amafoto 124,
ubona niba biteguye — kandi ubona raporo."*

## 6. Do NOT say (protects the position)

- ❌ "Turatsinda" / "we guarantee you pass" — we don't control the exam.
- ❌ "Ni app ya Polisi" / anything implying official status.
- ❌ "Abanyeshuri bacu bose batsinda 90%" — we have no pass-rate data yet. Say
  *"pilot"* and *"we will measure it"* instead.
- ❌ "Ishyura 5,000" — that is their price; it anchors us to them.
- ❌ Anything about their app by name in public marketing — compare on facts
  ("some apps charge before you see anything"), not on brands.

## 7. What makes the pitch true (backlog, in order)

The pitch above is honest **only if** these exist:

1. **Permit explainer page** — study → Irembo code → exam → 12/20 → RWF 5,000,
   plus *"we don't sell codes"*. Without it, gap #3 stays open and the honesty
   line has nothing to point at.
2. **PDF booklet** — gap #2 and #9 are the two most repeated requests. It is also
   the school handout and the WhatsApp forward.
3. **Instructor answer-key review** — the pitch promises 394 good questions. A
   school will test that in the first hour.
4. **Share-a-question card** — matches how their users already behave (53
   screenshots in 5 days).

Items 1 and 2 are content, not architecture — they don't touch the offline PWA.
