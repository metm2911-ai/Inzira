# Pilot Agreement — Inzira × Driving School

> Template only. Have a Rwandan lawyer review before signing. Not legal advice.

**Between:** ____________________________ ("the School"), a driving school registered in Rwanda, and **Inzira** ("the Provider"), represented by ____________________________.

**Date:** ____ / ____ / 20____
**Pilot period:** 8 weeks from the date of signature.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Provider gives the School free access to the Inzira theory-training app for up to **100 students** during the pilot. The School uses it with its students and shares anonymised results so both parties can measure whether it improves theory pass rates.

## 2. What the Provider delivers

1. Up to 100 student seats, activated within 5 working days.
2. A unique school code and a co-branded QR code for enrolment forms.
3. A short onboarding session for instructors (1–2 hours, on site or online).
4. A monthly report: seats used, mock exams taken, average score, predicted pass rate, weakest topics.
5. Technical support by phone/WhatsApp during business hours.

## 3. What the School delivers

1. Distributes the code/QR to students at enrolment.
2. Encourages each student to complete at least **3 mock exams** before the real test.
3. Shares the real theory results of participating students (pass/fail only, no national ID numbers) within 30 days of each exam.
4. Nominates one staff member as the contact person.
5. Gives feedback on question quality and usability.

## 4. Data and privacy

- Student progress is stored on the student's own device. The Provider does not collect names, phone numbers or national ID numbers in the pilot.
- The School will only share results in aggregate or with student consent.
- Both parties comply with Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 on personal data protection.
- The Provider may publish aggregate, anonymised results (e.g. "pilot pass rate: 82%") but will not identify the School without written permission.

## 5. Costs

Free during the pilot. After the pilot, the School may continue at the partner rate (see pricing sheet) with no obligation. **No automatic charges.**

## 6. Intellectual property

- The app, question bank, code and brand remain the Provider's property.
- The School's name and logo remain the School's property and are used only with permission.
- The School may not copy, resell or reverse-engineer the content.

## 7. No official endorsement

Inzira is an independent study aid. It is **not** endorsed by the Rwanda National Police (RNP) or any government body. Neither party will describe it as "official" or "RNP-approved".

## 8. Exclusivity

None. Both parties remain free to work with others. The School is not required to stop using any other tool.

## 9. Termination

Either party may end the pilot with 14 days' written notice (WhatsApp or email is acceptable). On termination, student seats stop at the end of the current month and the Provider deletes any student-level data it holds.

# Amasezerano y'igerageza — Inzira × Ishuri ry'ubushoferi

**Ari hagati ya:** ____________________________ ("Ishuri"), n'**Inzira** ("Utanga serivisi"), uhagarariwe na ____________________________.

**Itariki:** ____ / ____ / 20____
**Igihe cy'igerageza:** ibyumweru 8 uhereye ku itariki isezeranyweho.

## 1. Intego

Utanga serivisi aha Ishuri uburenganzira bwo gukoresha porogaramu Inzira ku banyeshuri **100** mu gihe cy'igerageza, nta kiguzi. Ishuri riyikoresha mu kwigisha, kandi impande zombi zipima niba yongera umubare w'abatsinda ikizamini cy'amategeko.

## 2. Ibyo Utanga serivisi azakora

1. Guha Ishuri imyanya 100 y'abanyeshuri mu minsi 5 y'akazi.
2. Kode y'ishuri yihariye na QR code ku ifishi yo kwiyandikisha.
3. Inama y'amasaha 1–2 ku barimu.
4. Raporo ya buri kwezi: imyanya yakoreshejwe, ibizamini byakozwe, amanota agereranyije, igipimo cyo gutsinda, n'ingingo zikomeye.
5. Ubufasha bwa telefone/WhatsApp mu masaha y'akazi.

## 3. Ibyo Ishuri rizakora

1. Rikwirakwiza kode/QR ku banyeshuri igihe biyandikisha.
2. Rishishikariza buri munyeshuri gukora nibura **ibizamini 3** mbere y'ikizamini nyakuri.
3. Rishyikiriza Utanga serivisi amanota y'ikizamini nyakuri (yatsinze/yatsinzwe gusa, nta nimero y'indangamuntu) mu minsi 30.
4. Ritora umukozi umwe wo kuba umuhuzabikorwa.
5. Ritanga ibitekerezo ku bibazo n'uburyo porogaramu ikoreshwa.

## 4. Amakuru n'ubuzima bwite

- Aho umunyeshuri ageze bibikwa kuri telefone ye. Utanga serivisi ntabika amazina, nimero ya telefone cyangwa indangamuntu mu gihe cy'igerageza.
- Ishuri ritanga amanota gusa, cyangwa ku bwumvikane bw'umunyeshuri.
- Impande zombi zubahiriza amategeko y'u Rwanda ku burenganzira bw'umuntu ku makuru (Law No. 058/2021).
- Utanga serivisi ashobora gutangaza imibare rusange itaranga Ishuri, ariko ntasohora izina ry'Ishuri nta bwumvikane.

## 5. Ikiguzi

Nta kiguzi mu gihe cy'igerageza. Nyuma y'igerageza, Ishuri rishobora gukomeza ku giciro cy'umufatanyabikorwa, nta gahato. **Nta kwishyura byikora.**

## 6. Uburenganzira bw'umutungo w'ubwenge

- Porogaramu, ibibazo, kode n'ikirango ni ibya Utanga serivisi.
- Izina n'ikirango by'Ishuri ni iby'Ishuri, kandi bikoreshwa gusa ku bwumvikane.
- Ishuri ntirishobora kwigana, kugurisha cyangwa gukora kopi y'ibiri mu porogaramu.

## 7. Nta kwemezwa na Leta

Inzira ni igikoresho cyigenga. **Ntabwo yemewe na Polisi y'Igihugu (RNP)** cyangwa ikindi kigo cya Leta. Nta mwanya uvuga ko ari "official" cyangwa "yemewe na RNP".

## 8. Ubwishingizi bw'isoko

Nta mwanya. Impande zombi zishobora gukorana n'abandi. Ishuri ntabwo rigomba kureka gukoresha ibindi bikoresho.

## 9. Guhagarika

Impande zombi zishobora guhagarika igerageza zimenyesheje indi iminsi 14 mbere (WhatsApp cyangwa email birahagije). Nimara guhagarikwa, imyanya ihagarara ku mpera y'ukwezi, kandi Utanga serivisi asiba amakuru yose y'abanyeshuri afite.
